Help for 3D Snake, Snake (No Ads) for Apple Vision Pro and Prashnavali.

Common questions

My score isn't showing on the Game Center leaderboard.

Make sure you are signed in to Game Center (Settings › Game Center) and connected to the internet. Scores earned while offline are submitted the next time you play while signed in.

Will I lose my best score if I delete the app?

Yes. Your best score is saved only on your device. Scores already sent to Game Center stay on the leaderboard.

Do the apps have ads or in-app purchases?

No. None of the apps show ads or sell anything inside the app.

Do the apps need an internet connection?

No. Everything works offline. The Snake games only use the internet to send scores to Game Center.

What data do the apps collect?

None. Each app's privacy policy explains the details: 3D Snake, Snake (No Ads), Prashnavali.
