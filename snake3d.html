Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Summary

3D Snake is a game made for everyone, including children. It does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game does not ask for names, email addresses, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

Data stored on your device

The game saves a few things only on your device so it remembers them between plays:

  • Your best score and last score
  • Game settings (speed, snake length, snake colour, music, sound effects, vibration, countdown)

This information never leaves your device. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Game Center

On iPhone and iPad, the game can use Apple's Game Center to show a high-score leaderboard. If you are signed in to Game Center, your best score is sent to Game Center so it can appear on the leaderboard. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer receives no personal information from Game Center. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in Settings, and parents can limit it with Screen Time.

Sharing your score

The game has a button to share your score. It opens Apple's standard share sheet, and nothing is shared unless you choose where to send it. The game does not receive anything back.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software, and it does not link out to other websites or apps except the Apple services described above.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). This meets the requirements of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws.
