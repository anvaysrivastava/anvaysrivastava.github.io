Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Summary

3D Snake does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your best score and game settings are saved on your device only. They are deleted when you delete the app.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your scores are sent to Game Center so they can appear on the leaderboard. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer receives no personal information from Game Center. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in Settings.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
