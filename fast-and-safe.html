Fast and Safe

For iPhone & iPad

Coming soon to the App Store

A racing game about going as fast as you dare without ending up in the ditch.

  • 50 levels of about 50 seconds each, through meadows, city streets and snow.
  • Loops, jumps, ice, hairpins and bridges that crumble if you slow down.
  • Race a ghost of your best run, and compete on a Game Center leaderboard for every level.
  • Play in portrait with touch controls, or in landscape with a game controller.
  • No ads.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Summary

Fast and Safe does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your best times, a replay of your best run on each level, the last level you played, your remaining cars for the day and whether you have an active subscription are saved on your device only. They are deleted when you delete the app.

Subscription

The optional Unlimited Cars subscription is sold and billed by Apple through your Apple ID. The developer never receives your name, email address or payment details. The game asks the App Store whether your subscription is active and remembers the answer on your device, so unlimited cars keep working offline. Purchases are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your best times are sent to Game Center so they can appear on the leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer receives no personal information from Game Center. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in Settings.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the game opens the standard iOS share sheet with a short message and a link to this page. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

Permissions

The game does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Unlimited Cars subscription

Without a subscription you get 10 cars a day; a car is used only when you end up in a ditch. Unlimited Cars is an optional auto-renewing monthly subscription that removes the limit. The price is shown in the game before you buy, and payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm. The subscription renews automatically each month unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage or cancel it at any time in your Apple ID's subscription settings. If you cancel, unlimited cars continue until the end of the period you have paid for.

Age Suitability

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Summary

Fast and Safe is a racing game made for players of all ages. There are no people to harm, no weapons, no frightening or mature content, no ads, no chat and no way to meet or message other players.

What players see

Players drive a small buggy through meadows, city streets and snow, against the clock. If the buggy ends up in the ditch, it bursts in a short cartoon explosion with a sound effect, and the player tries again. No characters are shown or hurt.

Other players

There is no chat, messaging, user-generated content or social feed. The only way players see each other is on optional Game Center leaderboards, which show Game Center nicknames and race times. Game Center is run by Apple, and parents can manage it with Screen Time.

Purchases

The game is free. Players get 10 cars a day; the optional Unlimited Cars subscription removes this limit. Purchases go through Apple, so Ask to Buy and Screen Time restrictions on in-app purchases apply. There are no loot boxes, random rewards or gambling of any kind.

Web and data

The game has no web browser. The Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links open in Safari. The game does not collect personal information; see the Privacy Policy.
